Delete your Doc Gen account

Doc Gen is made by Leon Digital. You can ask us to delete your Doc Gen account and its associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

In the app: open the menu → Account → Delete account, then send the email that opens.

Without the app: send an email to edvnzr@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Doc Gen account".

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and account ID.
  • Data linked to your account on our servers.

We delete it within 30 days of your request and reply to confirm.

What is not affected

  • Business records on your device (business profile, customers, documents, payments and PDFs) are never stored on our servers. They stay on your phone until you uninstall Doc Gen.
  • Backup files you saved yourself.

Questions: edvnzr@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
