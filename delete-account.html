Delete your Doc Gen account

Doc Gen is made by Leon Digital. You can delete your Doc Gen account and its associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

How to delete

In the app: open the menu → Account → Delete account, type "delete account" and confirm. If you signed in more than 10 minutes ago, you'll be asked to confirm it's you first.

On this page: sign in with the Google account you use in Doc Gen, type "delete account" and confirm.

Signed in as .

By email: if you signed in with an email code, or can't use the options above, email edvnzr@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Doc Gen account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and account ID.
  • Data linked to your account on our servers.

What is not affected

  • Business records on your device (business profile, customers, documents, payments and PDFs) are never stored on our servers. They stay on your phone until you uninstall Doc Gen.
  • Backup files you saved yourself.
  • Purchases, which belong to your Google Play account.

Questions: edvnzr@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
