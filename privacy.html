Doc Gen

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Leon Digital ("we", "us") makes Doc Gen, an app for creating quotations, invoices, receipts, credit notes, debit notes and refund notes. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It is written to meet Google Play's requirements and Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

Your business documents stay on your phone

Everything you enter in Doc Gen (your business details and logo, payment QR, customers, line items, documents, payments and the PDFs the app creates) is stored only on your device. We do not receive, see or store it.

It leaves your device only when you choose:

  • Sharing or printing a PDF hands it to the app or printer you pick.
  • Backups are files you save or share yourself (for example to your Downloads folder, Google Drive or email). Backups are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Information we do handle

What When Why Who processes it
Email address and an account ID Only if you sign in (Google or email link) To create your account and link your purchases to it Supabase (our backend provider, Singapore region) and Google if you use Google sign-in
Purchase records (product, order ID, status) When you buy an upgrade To unlock what you paid for and restore it on a new phone Google Play, Supabase
Advertising ID and device information In the free version, when ads are shown To show and measure ads Google AdMob
Crash reports (error details, device model, OS and app version) When the app crashes or hits an error To find and fix bugs Sentry

Crash reports do not include your business documents, customer details or amounts. Signing in is optional; without it, the app works fully offline and we receive no account information.

We do not sell your personal data.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show ads. Where required by law you are asked for consent, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads). Buying Remove Ads or Pro turns ads off.

See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Third-party services

  • Google Play (purchases): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (ads): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase (accounts): https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Sentry (crash reports): https://sentry.io/privacy/

Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app (backups you saved elsewhere are not affected).
  • Your account and purchase records are kept while you have an account. To delete your account and its data, email us at the address below from the account's email address; we delete it within 30 days. Purchase records that Google Play keeps are governed by Google's policy.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent. Contact us at the address below.

Security

Data sent to our backend and service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to account data is restricted so each account can only read its own records.

Children

Doc Gen is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and change the "Last updated" date above.
